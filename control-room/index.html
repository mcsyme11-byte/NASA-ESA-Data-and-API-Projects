The data-job standard

Every job fetches into the runner, then dev/datajob.py (run even when a step fails) checks each file: JSON must parse, a CSV must have a header and rows, no file may be emptied, a file over 2 KB may not shrink below 20% of its size, and no more than half of a folder may be deleted. Refused files are put back, a source that failed keeps its previous data, and the job writes shared/data/status/<job>.json and, when anything changed, commits as <Job>: … and pushes, retrying after git pull --rebase.
