How to read this: the rules

Size is the planet's radius when a transit measured it; otherwise its mass, which from radial velocity is a minimum (≥). Where neither was measured, the archive's estimate is shown and marked.

Year is the time the planet takes to go round its star once. Starlight is how much light and heat it gets compared with Earth: the archive's value, or L ÷ a² from the star's luminosity L and the orbit a.

Habitable zone: "Yes" when the starlight is between 0.359 and 1.108 of Earth's (NASA's rule); "Yes, with the star's temperature" when only the second rule, which allows for a cool or hot star's light, puts it inside. "Optimistic" marks planets inside only the wider, optimistic zone. "About Earth's size": a measured radius of 0.5 to 1.6 Earths, or, without one, a mass of 0.1 to 3 Earths.
