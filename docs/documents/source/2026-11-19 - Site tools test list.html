Site tools test list

The search, shortcuts, section links, preview cards, Lost in space and the Space Watch figures, added November 19, 2026
14Checks
~35 minIn total
3Sittings
AnyOrder

Tick each box when it works. If something looks wrong, write a word or two on its notes line and take a screenshot.

Part 1On a computerabout 20 minutes

  1. 1

    Search and shortcuts. On any page, press /, type “mars” and press Enter. Then press ? to see the list of shortcuts. On Orbit Watch, press 2 to open its second tab.

    Notes
  2. 2

    Copy link to this section. Point at a section heading and click its link icon. Paste the link into a new tab and check that it lands on that section.

    Notes
  3. 3

    Space Watch figures. Open the Space Watch page and check that all four figures show numbers.

    Notes
  4. 4

    Lost in space. Go to a wrong address. The Lost in space page should open, and its “Search the site” button should open the search with “nope” already typed in.

    Notes
  5. 5

    Orbit Watch. Turn and zoom the globe, tap a dot, search for one, and change the speed and the kind switches. Then try one close approach’s “Watch”, the collision calculator and the model.

    Notes
  6. 6

    Exoplanets. The light curves (including CHEOPS), “Make a transit” and the chart tooltips. Then the wobble, microlensing and the Webb picture. Then search for one planet, open its page with “Where it is in the sky”, and press Back.

    Notes
  7. 7

    Planetary Maps. The Globe, North and South views, the Moon and Mars tabs, the Apollo 11 and Jezero close-ups, a search by name and one by coordinates, and a place card’s link to Mars Data.

    Notes
  8. 8

    Mars Data. The dust storm section, humidity at the two sites, the rovers’ climbs, and the reload buttons.

    Notes
  9. 9

    Space Weather. The page’s reload button and one panel’s reload button. The “Since 2010” chart should mark the incomplete years.

    Notes
  10. 10

    Image Explorer. In the picture library, search “Saturn rings”, “hurricane” and “Apollo 11”, and note any poor top result. Then give the globe a quick look.

    Notes

Part 2On a phoneabout 10 minutes

  1. 11

    Phone layout. Look at Space Watch, Space Weather and the control room. Try “Search the site” in the footer, and tap a dot on the Orbit Watch globe.

    Notes
  2. 12

    Preview card. Send yourself the Space Watch link in Messages. The preview picture with the patch should appear.

    Notes
  3. 13

    New-tool dots. Come back the next day: the menu should show no dots, because nothing new was added after your first visit.

    Notes

Part 3Outdoors at nightabout 5 minutes

  1. 14

    Sky Finder. Calibrate on the Moon, check that the markers line up with what you see, and switch the target.

    Notes
